# RoboFang: Neglected but Interesting

Repo: [robofang](https://github.com/sandraschi/robofang) (hub :10870, bridge :10871, supervisor :10872). Status v1.8.0 Beta, rapid iteration, honestly so. Neglected, not dead - and the older sibling of today's plans.

---

## The concept

A secure command center connecting AI agent fleets to the physical world: smart home, professional software, robotic hardware - local-first, privacy-first, safety-gated. Federated mesh architecture (nodes bridged over Tailscale), foreman/labor/audit cognitive loop, skill "hands" for specific tools, filesystem-shadow sandbox (Dark Twin) for safe staging, robot safety protocols with hazard docs, and docs for the Noetix Bumi humanoid and the Yahboom baseline alike. Even a decommissioned Snark T-800 with critical safety warnings - this repo has lore.

## Relation to sandrafleetbot

RoboFang tried the command-center idea first: board-like channels, agent comms, safety logic, hub-and-spoke tool routing. sandrafleetbot ([Coming Next](COMING_NEXT.md)) is the second attempt with the full fleet behind it and the local brain ([Muse Glimmer](LOCAL_LLM_STACK.md)) making the economics work. Patterns flow forward: mission loop safety, federation thinking, the honesty contract (planned integrations marked planned, e.g. DefenseClaw/OpenShell/Bastio). If SFB is the product, RoboFang is the scar tissue - read its philosophy and safety docs before designing v2 of anything.
